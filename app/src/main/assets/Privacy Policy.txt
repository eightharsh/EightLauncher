eight privacy information
Maintainer: eightharsh

This launcher contains no analytics SDK, remote crash reporter, performance-monitoring SDK, Firebase integration, or push messaging service.

Settings, favorite/hidden app choices, and launcher-recorded screen-time sessions are stored locally. Screen-time records reflect sessions initiated through the launcher, rather than a full system-wide usage audit. Android may include app data in backups according to device settings and the app backup rules. Uninstalling removes local app data; platform backups are managed separately.

Downloadable Google Fonts use the Google Play Services font provider; bundled fonts do not require a download. Google Play Services operates under their own privacy policies.

The launcher queries installed apps to display and open them. Work and Private Space integration uses Android profile APIs. Optional accessibility access performs the screen-lock action for double-tap to lock; the service does not process accessibility event contents.

Widgets and apps opened through the launcher operate independently and may access the network or collect data under their own policies.

Quick notes, checklist state, and reminder times are stored in the app's local preferences. Optional reminders use Android WorkManager and local notifications, not a cloud service. Completing or deleting a task cancels its pending reminder. Battery restrictions can delay notifications.

Backups are exported only to a file you select through Android's file picker. They contain settings, app customization choices, and notes in plain text. Restoring a backup replaces the supported preferences and notes after confirmation.
